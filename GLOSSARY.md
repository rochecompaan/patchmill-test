# Team Lunch Poll

Team Lunch Poll helps a team choose a meal or restaurant. This glossary describes one shared lunch-poll context.

## Language

**Poll**:
A question about lunch, its available options, and the votes for those options.
_Avoid_: Survey, ballot (for the whole poll)

**Option**:
One meal or restaurant that a teammate can choose in a poll.
_Avoid_: Choice (as an entity name), candidate

**Vote**:
One anonymous selection of one option in a poll. A vote does not identify a teammate.
_Avoid_: Voter, reaction

**Vote count**:
The number of votes for one option. An option without votes has a vote count of zero.
_Avoid_: Score, stored total
