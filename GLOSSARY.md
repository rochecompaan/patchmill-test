# Team Lunch Poll

This context helps a team choose where or what to eat for lunch.

## Language

**Poll**:
A lunch question with its options and votes.

**Option**:
One lunch choice in a poll, such as a meal or a restaurant.

**Vote**:
One selection of one option in a poll. A vote is not a vote count or a record of who voted.

**Vote count**:
The number of votes for one option in a poll.
